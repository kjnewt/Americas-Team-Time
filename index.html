Why this matters

On every project, risk starts on the jobsite and grows each time work changes hands. Customers who feel understood stop shopping for point solutions. Today’s activity is a role play built on emotional intelligence: first you live the project as your customer, then you respond as their Trimble team.

Challenges 1 and 2

You are the customer

Step into their role. Five pairs each take one stakeholder card and speak for that person’s pressure. Notice what they would never say in a discovery call, and see the risk the way they do.

Challenge 3

You are their Trimble team

Step back into your own role. Use what you felt to answer their concerns with empathy, tell The Trimble Story in their language, and earn the next meeting from your partner table.

Challenge 3 · Make the Case

Tell the Trimble Story beyond point solutions

Strong answers

Fall short when
